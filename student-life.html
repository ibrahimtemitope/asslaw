Life at Asslaw

Clubs, creativity, collaboration, and the kind of discipline that builds character.

Our Clubs & Societies

Every student finds their tribe at Asslaw. Our clubs are spaces where passions ignite and friendships last a lifetime.

🚀

JETS Club

Junior Engineers, Technicians and Scientists. For the innovators who dream in circuits, code, and chemical reactions. JETS members lead our Science Fair Fridays.

📰

Press Club

The voice of Asslaw. Student journalists cover school events, publish newsletters, and learn the power of storytelling and responsible media.

🏠

Home Maker Club

Practical life skills meet creativity. From cooking to home management, this club prepares students for independent, organized living.

🎭

Drama & Arts

Expression through performance. Students write, direct, and act in plays that explore themes of morality, community, and growth.

⚽

Sports & Athletics

Physical fitness, teamwork, and healthy competition. Our sports programs build bodies and bonds that last beyond the field.

🌱

Agriculture Club

Connecting students to the earth. Learn sustainable farming, crop management, and the value of growing your own food.

Science Fair Fridays

Every Friday, Asslaw transforms into an exhibition of imagination. Students are challenged to create something extraordinary—with the sky as their only limit.

Some projects seem impossible at first. Some students struggle to deliver. But when they finally do, it feels like a masterpiece. And the teachers? They celebrate every single attempt.

This isn't just about science. It's about resilience, creativity, and learning that failure is just practice for success.

Discipline with Love

At Asslaw, when a student does something wrong, they aren't shamed—they're guided. Our discipline is immediate, fair, and rooted in genuine care.

We have no room for bad conduct, but we have endless room for growth. Every correction is an opportunity to build a better character.

Students don't fear authority here. They respect it because they know it comes from love.
